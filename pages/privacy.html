Privacy Policy

SentryEye is built on a foundational promise: we cannot access your data because we never receive it.

// Overview

✓ SentryEye's developers have ZERO access to your personal data. This is an architectural guarantee, not a policy promise.

SentryEye is a local-first Android security application. Its core operations execute entirely within the boundaries of your device.

// Data Processing & Storage

Because SentryEye operates locally, your data is handled with strict isolation:

  • Local Execution: All security monitoring, log analysis, and threat detection algorithms run locally on your device's CPU/GPU.
  • No Cloud Storage: We do not operate external databases, cloud storage servers, or remote logging systems to collect your information.
  • Data Retention: Security logs and history generated by the app are stored exclusively in your device's secure private storage. Deleting the app permanently erases all associated data.

// Android Permissions Explained

To provide robust security monitoring, SentryEye requires certain system permissions. Here is exactly how they are used locally:

Permission Why It's Needed Privacy Guarantee
Storage / Media To scan files for local vulnerabilities and malware. Files are analyzed in-memory; no files are ever uploaded.
Accessibility Service To detect malicious app behaviors and overlay attacks. No keystrokes or personal texts are ever read or saved.
Notification Access To block high-risk phishing links sent via messaging apps. Processed instantly on-device and immediately discarded.

// Third-Party Services & Analytics

We believe in absolute transparency regarding third-party integrations:

  • No Telemetry: We do not use third-party analytics frameworks (like Firebase Analytics) that track your behavior or identity.
  • Crash Reporting: If the app crashes, anonymized system logs may be sent via the Google Play Console strictly to fix bugs. No PII is included.

// Your Rights & Control

Since we do not hold your data, you possess ultimate control over your information:

You can instantly revoke any permission granted to SentryEye through your Android System Settings at any time. Doing so may limit certain features, but the app will never penalize you for securing your device.

// Changes to This Policy

Our core philosophy will never change: your data will always remain on your device. Updates to fix technical details will be posted here with a revised date.
